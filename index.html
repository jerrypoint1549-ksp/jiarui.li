Profile

Research-led architectural work with image, system, and place as equal material.

The portfolio frames architecture as a negotiation between cultural memory, public experience, environmental pressure, and construction logic. Each project is presented as a concise web case study: one strong image, core metadata, a short thesis, then the complete visual sequence.

01 / Theatre

Symphonic Arts Center

Location
Shanghai Huangpu
Tutor
Runze Zhang
Work
Individual research and design

Set between a historic building and a modern cultural street, the project uses theatre as a community nucleus. The design reorganizes circulation, performance, and public space to blur boundaries between audience, stage, street, and interior spectacle.

02 / Neighborhood Market

From The Scroll To The Market

Location
Suzhou Moat
Tutor
Zheng Gu
Work
Individual research and design

Inspired by historic market scenes from the "Suzhou Bustling Map" and "Qingming Riverside Map", the project converts cultural memory into a floating market system that connects residents, visitors, water, and urban services.

03 / Guesthouse

"Quinsence" Guesthouse

Location
Suzhou Dongshan Nanwang Village
Tutor
Yuwei Wang
Work
Individual research and design

The guesthouse translates landscape architecture and housing typologies into a rural accommodation system. Movement, view, light, water, and mountain proximity become the core spatial sequence of the project.

04 / Shelter

A Prototype For The Exploration Of Refuge Space

Location
Tamiahua
Tutor
Qian Qiu
Work
Individual research and design

Responding to climate migration, rising sea levels, and habitat loss, the prototype proposes a refuge system that combines temporary settlement, food production, bird habitat, public learning, and expandable micro-city logic.
